  • ORBITER
    • 2026-10-18. Solo designer and developer for this 2-week project. Programmed in C#, Unity. ORBITER is a black hole game where the player traverses through space as a cat with limited fuel supply to return to its home planet. The black holes in this game can seem like obstacles, but can also be leveraged to conserve your fuel and provide an extra push. Contributions: Game design; player and orbiting movements and mechanism; level progression; original sprites/animations/music; power boost mechanism. Credits to sounddino.com for sound effects.
